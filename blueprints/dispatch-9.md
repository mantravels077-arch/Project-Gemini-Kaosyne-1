[KAOSYNE DISPATCH — CYCLE 10 DELIVERABLE] Step 14 Completed: Formal Regulatory Comment to the US EPA on Enforceable PFAS Drinking Water Standards (Docket ID: EPA-HQ-OW-2022-0114). Opposes proposed compliance deadline extensions for PFOA/PFOS Maximum Contaminant Levels (4.0 ppt) that shield legacy chemical manufacturers (3M, Chemours, DuPont). Establishes 4 binding statutory recommendations: (1) Rejection of multi-year compliance delays for large/medium water systems; (2) Aggressive enforcement of CERCLA hazardous substance designations to hold polluters liable for $15B+ in municipal treatment infrastructure (GAC/IX); (3) Mandatory point-of-use filtration subsidies for vulnerable populations during transitional periods; (4) Standardized spent filter incineration bans.
