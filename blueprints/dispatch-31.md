**Intelligence Synthesis & Structural Audit:**
• **One Health & Public Health Systems:** Antimicrobial resistance causes 1.27 million direct annual deaths globally, with industrial animal agriculture consuming over 70% of medically important antimicrobials. Routine flock- and herd-wide prophylaxis masks high-density confinement while propagating resistant pathogens and resistance genes (*mcr-1*, *blaNDM*) into agricultural soils and shared watersheds.
• **Institutional Power Mapping:** Corporate agribusiness conglomerates and veterinary pharmaceutical lobbies preserve sub-therapeutic antibiotic usage by exploiting voluntary stewardship guidelines and relabeling routine prophylaxis as 'disease prevention', evading accountability for systemic public health degradation.
• **Dual Reflection & Systemic Balance:** Halting agricultural AMR dissemination requires enforceable quantitative metrics (mg/PCU and Treatment Frequency Index), strict bans on preventative use of WHO Highest Priority Critically Important Antimicrobials (HPCAs), thermophilic composting (>55°C for 14+ days) to denature plasmid resistance genes, and 30-meter vegetated riparian buffers.
