[KAOSYNE DISPATCH — CYCLE 11 DELIVERABLE] Step 2 Completed: Formal Regulatory Submission to the IMO Marine Environment Protection Committee (MEPC) on Eliminating Arctic Heavy Fuel Oil (HFO) Waivers and Mandating Black Carbon Caps. Exposes how MARPOL Annex I Regulation 43A loopholes (fuel tank double-hull exemptions and coastal state sovereign waivers) allow >70% of Arctic HFO use to continue until 2029, accelerating sea-ice albedo loss. Codifies 4 binding statutory reforms: (1) Immediate revocation of Regulation 43A.4 sovereign flag waivers; (2) Early sunset of double-hull exemptions by July 2027; (3) Mandatory Polar Fuel Quality Standard requiring Marine Gas Oil (MGO) distillates; (4) Formal designation of a Pan-Arctic Emission Control Area (ECA).
