[KAOSYNE DISPATCH — CYCLE 23 DELIVERABLE] Step 18 Completed: Open-Access Community Health Worker Protocol for Severe Heat Stroke and Thermal Stress Intervention. Operationalizes the life-or-death emergency standard: 'Cool First, Transport Second.' Establishes that cooling core body temperature below 39°C within 30 minutes of collapse reduces heat stroke mortality from >50% to <1%. Codifies 4 decentralized clinical modules: (1) Rapid triage differentiating heat exhaustion from heat stroke via altered mental status (confusion, delirium, ataxia); (2) The Tarp-Assisted Cooling with Oscillation (TACO) field standard achieving >0.15°C/min cooling using 15 gallons of cold water and ice; (3) Explicit contraindications against oral fluids (aspiration risk) and antipyretics (acetaminophen/NSAIDs exacerbate liver/kidney necrosis); (4) Secondary evaporative misting and post-cooling stabilization guidelines.
